        Tap squares to make a beat. Tap a name to hear it. The beat keeps playing when you switch to Kalimba, so you can play along.

        Every chord fits the key, so any order sounds good — try I – V – vi – IV. With the beat on, chords snap into time. “Play on the beat” repeats a chord on every beat until you tap another.
