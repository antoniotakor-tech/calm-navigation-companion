# Ad copy — Calm Companion launch

Three angles, each in English and Dutch. Limits: headline ≤ 40 characters, primary text ≤ 125 characters (character counts in brackets, checked by script).

Tone rules: speak to the feeling ("the city feels like too much"), never name a medical condition, plain everyday words, confident.

Call to action: use the platform button **Learn more** (NL: **Meer informatie**). The link goes to the landing page; use the matching URL from `tracking-links.csv`.
