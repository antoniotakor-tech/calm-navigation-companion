# Video scripts — three 15-second vertical ads (9:16)

**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 15 s, no voice-over (most people watch with sound off). Burned-in text on every shot.
**Recording:** iPhone screen recording (Control Centre → Screen Recording) of the live app in **Light** mode. Turn on Do Not Disturb first so no notifications show up. Clean status bar: full battery if possible, Wi-Fi on.
**No real people.** Only the app screen, doodles and Amanda.
**Audio:** use the app's own sounds (Rain, Stream, the kalimba), recorded with the screen recording, so there's no music licence to worry about. Keep it soft and fade it in. Nothing sudden or loud.
**Editing:** CapCut or Canva (free). Upload the clips, add the text, export 1080 × 1920.

## Safe zones (keep text inside)

| Platform | Keep clear | In pixels (1080 × 1920) |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6% | top 270, bottom 670, left/right 65 |
| Instagram Stories | top and bottom 14% | top 270, bottom 270 |
| TikTok | top 8%, bottom 25%, right 12% (buttons) | top 150, bottom 480, right 140 |

**Simple rule that works on all three:** put text between **y = 300 and y = 1250**, and at least **140 px** from the right edge. Text size at least 56 px, dark ink on cream or white, the hand-drawn font (Gaegu, free on Google Fonts).

**Captions:** Instagram and TikTok both offer auto-captions. Turn them on only if there's speech; these videos have none. The on-screen text below *is* the caption.

## Video A · "The calmer route" (15 s)

| Time | Screen to record | On-screen text (EN / NL) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Route tab, map of central Amsterdam, empty "Where to?" box | **The city feels like too much?** / **Voelt de stad als te veel?** |
| 2–5 s | Type a real destination (e.g. "Vondelpark") and tap **Find calm route** | **Tell it where you're going** / **Zeg waar je heen gaat** |
| 5–9 s | The result card: calm route drawn on the map, **Calm 8/10** next to the fast route | **It finds the quieter way** / **Hij vindt de rustigere weg** |
| 9–12 s | Tap **Start**. First direction, then tap **Sounds → Rain** in the navigation bar | **Add some rain if you like** / **Zet wat regen aan** |
| 12–15 s | End card: Amanda + logo on cream paper | **Calm Companion. The calm navigation app.** + "Link in bio" / "Link in bio" |

Audio: rain from the app, fading in at 9 s.

## Video B · "The map that feels the weather" (15 s)

| Time | Screen to record | On-screen text (EN / NL) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Route tab, zoomed in so the doodle clouds and sun drift across the map | **Your map can see the sky** / **Je kaart ziet de lucht** |
| 3–7 s | Slow pan over the map: clouds, sun, the little snail, flowers | **The doodles follow the real weather** / **De doodles volgen het echte weer** |
| 7–11 s | Switch to Dark mode (moon button) to show the evening look, then back | **Day and night, too** / **Ook dag en nacht** |
| 11–15 s | End card: Amanda + logo | **A calmer map to look at.** + "Link in bio" |

Tip: record on a day with clouds or rain, so the doodles are lively. Audio: Stream sound from the app.
