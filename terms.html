Community rules

  • Treat everyone with respect. Many people here are sensitive to stress — keep it calm.
  • No hateful, violent, sexual or bullying content.

Purchases in the iPhone app are handled by Apple under its own terms; on the web by our payment provider. Because it is digital content delivered immediately, you agree that the 14-day withdrawal right ends once Calm+ is unlocked, except where the law gives you more rights.

"Support the maker" tips are voluntary one-time gifts. They unlock nothing and are not refundable except where the law says otherwise.

Safety and health

Calm routes, sounds and tools are meant to make everyday moments easier. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always use your own judgement on the street, follow traffic rules, and contact a professional or emergency services if you need help.

Maps, places and timetables

Maps and places come from OpenStreetMap contributors; timetables from Transitous and public-transport operators; weather from Open-Meteo; place photos from Wikimedia and Panoramax contributors. They can be incomplete or out of date.
