Privacy

Calm Navigation Companion · last updated 12 October 2026

The short version

  • Accounts are optional and use passkeys (no password, no email). There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers in this app.
  • Your location is used to show you on the map and to guide you. It is not stored on our servers and not sold or shared for advertising.
  • Your recent routes and saved places stay on your device only.
  • Things stored on a server only if you choose them: a Calm Echo, your community profile and posts, an optional passkey sign-in, and Calm+ sync (details below).

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show your position, bias search results towards where you are, and give turn-by-turn directions.

WhenWhat happens
You focus a search box, or tap "show my location"One location fix is used to find your city for better search results and to centre the map.
You search for a place or routeThe coordinates needed for that request are sent to our routing provider (GraphHopper) through this app's own server so the key stays private. Search text and map areas are also looked up through free open-data services (OpenStreetMap-based).
You are navigatingLocation fixes are processed on your device. Only if the app needs to find a new route (you went off-route) is one current point sent to the routing provider.
Navigating with the screen locked (iPhone/Android app only)Only if you turn on "keep navigating when locked" and allow location "Always". Your phone shows its own indicator while this is active. You can stop at any time with Stop, or revoke permission in your phone's Settings.

What stays on your device

Your last 5 routes, places you pin, and your sound and display settings are saved in your device's own storage. They are never sent to our servers. Clear them in Settings → Saved places, or by clearing the app's data.

Calm Echoes (optional)

A Calm Echo is a short melody and an optional short message that you can leave at a place for other people to find. If you save one, the following is stored on our server (Cloudflare):

  • an approximate location (deliberately blurred, not your exact position),
  • the melody, your optional message and optional nickname,
  • the time it was left, and a random anonymous code stored on your device so you can recognise your own Echoes. This is not linked to your name or any account.

Echoes are shown to people nearby for 48 hours. Don't put personal information in a message — other people can read it.

Community profile and posts (optional)

If you create a profile we store the name, short bio and flower you pick, and an optional profile picture (a photo of yourself or a drawing, shrunk to a small square on your phone and visible to everyone in Community). Posts you share (a calm spot, route or event) are stored with their title, text, place, route line, event time and optional photo, and anyone using the app can see them. Photos are shrunk on your phone first, which also removes hidden location data (EXIF). Please don’t post photos of other recognisable people without their OK. Your profile is linked to a random key on your device; we store only a one-way hash of it. You can delete any post, or delete your whole profile and all posts, from the Community tab.

Passkey sign-in (optional)

A passkey is created and kept by your device (for example in iCloud Keychain) and unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or your device PIN. We receive and store only its public key, a counter, and when it was created and last used. We never see your face, fingerprint or PIN. Sign-in sessions are random tokens we store only as hashes; they expire after 180 days. "Delete account" removes the account, passkeys, sessions, profile and posts.

Calm+ and payments (optional)

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. We store your Stripe customer ID, your plan and when it renews. With Calm+, we also store a copy of your saved places, calm ratings, settings and blocked people so they sync between your devices. It's overwritten every time you sync and deleted with your account. Calm+ is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Problem reports

If you send a report from Settings, we store your message, the optional email you give, the app version, the screen you were on and your device type (never your location), to fix problems. Ask us to delete it any time.

Who else sees data

  • GraphHopper receives coordinates only as part of a search or routing request, to answer it. Its own privacy policy applies.
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap services supply map tiles and place data.
  • Cloudflare hosts the app and, like any web host, sees requests in transit. We don't add analytics or log your coordinates.
  • Open-Meteo receives your approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) to show the weather on the map. No account or ID is sent.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons / Panoramax receive the position of a place you tap on (never your own position) to show its photo and description.
  • Stripe processes Calm+ payments under its own privacy policy.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. Search still works without it, just without nearby ranking.
  • Delete saved places and recents inside the app, or clear the app's data.
  • To remove an Echo you left, or to report one you found, email the address below with where it is and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
